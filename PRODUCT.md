# Marbles

## Register
product

## Users
Office workers who want to delegate everyday tasks and spend less time operating a computer. Some have low confidence with software or tire quickly from sustained screen, mouse, keyboard, or mid-air gesture use.

## Product Purpose
Let people direct a visible team through voice and hand gestures. The current runtime assigns a task to one supported local agent. Present real task progress, results and produced files, with technical activity available on request. Agent collaboration remains a future capability.

## Brand Personality
Calm, precise, cinematic. Use one dark holographic agent space with a glowing voice orb and gesture control. The user wants a restrained JARVIS-inspired interface, with no cute characters or office scenery, no dashboard tabs, no typed composer and no button bars.

## Anti-references
Avoid a developer console as the default, unexplained floating controls, mandatory gestures, automatically enabled microphones, disappearing results, dashboards and navigation tabs, and technical logs presented as task summaries.

## Design Principles
Keep the canvas as the only home surface. Make the next action explicit. Keep work in stable locations. Reveal technical detail on request. Give gesture targets generous size, clear labels, and stable positions; keep mouse and keyboard access as an accessibility fallback.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Readable text, generous targets, visible focus, reduced motion, explicit open and close actions, responsive layouts, and status words alongside color. Voice and camera are opt-in. Use semantic landmarks and native form controls.
